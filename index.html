SURVEY CORPS MANUAL

FIELD GUIDE

ODM GRAPPLING

Your steel cables are your primary engine. Anchor high onto eaves, clocktowers, or trees with Q / E for pendulum swings, or hold Right-Click for twin-cable pull.

Hold Space to winch-reel rapidly along taut cables. Release at the upward apex of a fast swing to trigger a slingshot launch.

MOMENTUM BOOST

Gas Boost (Shift + WASD) amplifies your existing swing speed and smoothly curves your trajectory toward where you steer.

Build speed with cables first, then engage gas to carve through streets and line up high-velocity nape strikes. Hold S + Shift to air-brake.

COMBAT & SQUAD

Aim near a Titan's nape to engage soft-lock. Faster approaches deal exponentially higher blade damage. Hold Left-Click mid-air for a Spiral Slash.

Use Thunder Spears [X] to shatter Armored plating, and issue Squad Orders [T] to call focus attacks, Garrison cannons, or field resupplies.
